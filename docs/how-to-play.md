# How to Play

A dungeon speedrun. Race through **11 rooms**, kill the **boss** at the end, and post your time to the leaderboard. Lower is better.

## Goal

Clear every enemy in a room to unlock its door. Go through the door. Repeat until the boss is dead.

## Controls

| Key            | Action            |
| -------------- | ----------------- |
| WASD / arrows  | Move              |
| Mouse          | Aim               |
| Left click     | Shoot             |
| `1` / `2` / `3` | Switch weapon    |
| `E`            | Pick up / interact |
| `I`            | Open inventory    |
| `Enter`        | Use selected item |
| `Esc`          | Pause             |

## Weapons

| Slot | Weapon  | Notes                          |
| ---- | ------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1    | Pistol  | Fast, low damage.              |
| 2    | Rifle   | Balanced.                      |
| 3    | Shotgun | High damage, slow fire rate.   |

Each weapon has its own cooldown between shots, and there's a brief cooldown when you swap weapons. Ammo is tracked per weapon — pick up ammo crates to refill.

## Enemies

| Enemy    | Threat                                              |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Grunt    | Baseline zombie. Patrols until you're close.        |
| Runner   | Fast and frail. Closes the gap quickly.             |
| Tank     | Slow, tough, armored.                               |
| Kamikaze | Rushes you and explodes on contact. Keep your distance. |
| Boss     | Room 11 only. Armored. Finish it to finish the run. |

Enemies only chase you once you're inside their aggro range — you can sometimes walk past them or pick off patrols before they react.

## Pickups

Walk up and press `E` to grab them.

| Pickup        | Effect                                               |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Health potion | Heals you.                                           |
| Ammo crate    | Refills ammo for your current weapon.                |

## Inventory

Open with `I`. You can hold items picked up during the run and use them when it matters:

- **Health potion** — heal.
- **Speed boost** — short burst of speed.
- **Shield** — reduces incoming damage for a few seconds.

## Rooms

- Rooms are single-screen arenas.
- A door stays locked until **every enemy** in the room is dead.
- You progress in order — Room 1 → Room 2 → ... → Room 11.
- Dying restarts the run.

## Leaderboard

When you finish, your time goes on the public leaderboard. Shave seconds off however you can — **be creative**.

Good luck.
